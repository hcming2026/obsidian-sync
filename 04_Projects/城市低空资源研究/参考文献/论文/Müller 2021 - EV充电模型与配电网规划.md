## 摘要
> 摘要来源：Semantic Scholar（已核验）
>
> The expected electrification of the transportation sector and the associated expansion of charging infrastructure will represent a challenge for the electricity distribution grid due to the additional load. For this reason, many studies investigate the influence of electric vehicle charging on the distribution grid. However, these studies make different assumptions for modelling the charging processes. This paper investigates the impact of modelling assumptions for charging processes of electric vehicles on the distribution grid. Here, a simplified charging process modelling is compared with a comprehensive charging process modelling. The comprehensive modelling focuses on the representation of charging losses as well as the charging method of the battery system. Whereas the simplified modelling does not consider these parameters. The influence of the charging process modelling on the grid is determined by calculating the simultaneity factors for synthetically generated charging profiles. The investigation shows that simplified modelling results in a higher load caused by electric vehicles compared to comprehensive modelling. A simplified modelling approach is therefore sufficient
