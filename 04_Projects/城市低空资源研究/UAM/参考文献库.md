
# UAM 垂直起降场充电负荷与模型精细度 · 参考文献库

> 整理目的：支撑“如何科学决定模型精细度”这篇基础方法型论文，案例为垂直起降场充电负荷。
> 说明：除“待核对”部分外，DOI 均已核验。使用前建议再核对卷期/页码及个别作者全名。

## 1. 模型精细度与模型复杂度（方法论核心）

- `Priesmann J, Nolting L, Praktiknjo A. Are complex energy system models more accurate? An intra-model comparison of power system optimization models. Applied Energy, 2019, 255: 113783.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2019.113783  
  用途：复杂度—精度边际收益递减的直接证据，适合作为方法论主引用。

- `Wirtz M, Hahn M, Schreiber T, Müller D. Design optimization of multi-energy systems using mixed-integer linear programming: Which model complexity and level of detail is sufficient? Energy Conversion and Management, 2021.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enconman.2021.114249  
  用途：研究问题几乎与本论文相同，是“最低够用复杂度”框架的最近对标。

- `Gils H C, Gardian H, Kittel M, et al. Modeling flexibility in energy systems — comparison of power sector models based on simplified test cases. Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews, 2022, 158: 111995.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2021.111995  
  用途：高影响力综述型模型对比，证明“同问题、不同细节层级”的研究范式成立。

- `Nigischer C, Reiterer F, Bougain S, Grafinger M. Finding the proper level of detail to achieve sufficient model fidelity using FlexSim: An industrial use case. Procedia CIRP, 2023, 119: 1240–1245.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.procir.2023.02.192  
  用途：近期“足够保真即可”的实证案例，用于说明研究空白仍未完全闭合。

- `Madan M S, Son Y J, Cho H, Kulvatunyou B. Determination of efficient simulation model fidelity for flexible manufacturing systems. International Journal of Computer Integrated Manufacturing, 2005.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/0951192052000288143  
  用途：早期经典文献，用于交代模型精细度选择问题的历史沿革。

- `Daganzo C F, Gayah V V, Gonzales E J. The potential of parsimonious models for understanding large scale transportation systems and answering big picture questions. EURO Journal on Transportation and Logistics, 2012, 1(1–2): 47–65.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s13676-012-0003-z  
  用途：交通领域“简约模型”的方法论宣言，说明“够用即可”在交通建模中已有思想传统。

- `Peinturier L, Wallom D. Building energy simulation calibration accuracy and modelling complexity: Implications for energy performance improvement. Energy and Buildings, 2025.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2025.115971  
  用途：用敏感度分析判断“哪些模型简化可接受、哪些必须保留”，可直接迁移到你的判断流程。

- `Schricker B C, Franceschini R W, et al. Fidelity Evaluation Framework. Proceedings of the IEEE Annual Simulation Symposium, 2001: 109–116.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/SIMSYM.2001.922122  
  用途：早期“保真度评价框架”，适合做方法论谱系起点。

- `Müller T, Ali S A, Becker M, et al. Impact of different electric vehicle charging models on distribution grid planning. CIRED, 2021.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1049/icp.2021.1858  
  用途：直接比较“简化充电模型 vs 完整充电模型”对电网规划的影响，和你的 F0–F4 对比思路一致。

## 2. 低空交通网络与管理（低空研究方向）

- `Fu R, Safadi Y, Quan Q, Haddad J. Sky highway: An air traffic structure for low-altitude heterogeneous VTOL aircraft. Transportation Research Part C, 2026, 185: 105531.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2026.105531  
  用途：低空“空中高速路”网络结构，TRC 最新低空交通组织方法。

- `Weng C, Pan T, Chen C, Zhong R. Urban low-altitude air transport management: Bridging dynamic traffic control and static network equilibrium. Transportation Research Part C, 2025, 178: 105237.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2025.105237  
  用途：城市低空交通管理的动静态模型衔接，最贴近 TRC 框架的低空论文之一。

- `Kitthamkesorn S, Chen A. Maximum capture problem for urban air mobility network design. Transportation Research Part E, 2024, 187: 103569.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tre.2024.103569  
  用途：UAM 网络设计的设施捕获模型，作为案例侧供给侧方法参照。

- `He X, Li L, Mo Y, Huang J, Qin S J. A distributed route network planning method with congestion pricing for drone delivery services in cities. Transportation Research Part C, 2024, 160: 104536.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2024.104536  
  用途：低空物流航路网络规划，反衬充电负荷精细度问题仍少人研究。

## 3. 低空 / UAM / 机场充电需求与电网

- `Wu J, Cao S, Hansen M, González M C. Integrating urban air mobility into the power grid through smart charging solutions. Transportation Research Part C, 2025, 179: 105281.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2025.105281  
  用途：TRC 顶刊直接研究 UAM 充电需求与电网，是最主要对标论文。

- `Fan P, Bu S, Zhu M, Wen Y, Li S, Zhang G, Zhang C. Analysis of multi-modal energy supply and power systems adaptability for eVTOL and UAV applications in the low-altitude economy. eTransportation, 2026, 29: 100626.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.etran.2026.100626  
  用途：明确以“low-altitude economy”为对象，做多模态能源供应与电力适配，是案例最直接的对标。

- `Fan P, Bu S, Li S, Wen Y. Empowering short-haul aviation electrification: Insights into load characterization, grid adaptability, and social welfare. eTransportation, 2026, 28: 100585.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.etran.2026.100585  
  用途：负荷特征刻画 + 电网适配 + 社会福利，方法可借鉴。

- `Qian T, Yue J, Wang C, Hu Q, Hui H. Urban energy and three-dimensional mobility integration for eVTOL scale-up. Applied Energy, 2026, 421: 128226.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2026.128226  
  用途：把垂直起降场定义为“能源—交通耦合节点”，支撑问题设定。

- `Chen S K, Shamshiripour A, Seshadri R, et al. Potential short- to long-term impacts of on-demand urban air mobility on transportation demand in North America. Transportation Research Part A, 2024, 190: 104288.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tra.2024.104288  
  用途：批评现有模型忽略充电、场内活动与容量，是研究空白的直接证据。

- `Li Y, et al. Electrifying regional mobility: Planning intercity low-altitude air routes for Chinese cities. The Innovation Energy, 2025, 2(4): 100113.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.59717/j.xinn-energy.2025.100113  
  用途：低空航线电能需求分析，兼顾中国场景与基础设施需求。

- `Coenen T, Malarkey A, MacKenzie D. Estimating Electrical Energy and Capacity Demand for Regional Electric Flight Operations at Two Mid-Size Airports in Washington, U.S. Transportation Research Record, 2024, 2678(6): 911–925.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/03611981231201110  
  用途：机场级电量/容量需求估算，用于和“精细化到哪一步”形成对比。

- `Dong H, Chung C-Y, Chen Y, et al. Evaluating flight-aware charging for electric aircraft under airport-specific pricing. Transport Policy, 2026, 186: 104229.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tranpol.2026.104229  
  用途：电动飞机“航班感知充电”与机场定价，补充机场充电策略模型。

- `Han H, Song B D. Rolling horizon optimization of urban air mobility (UAM) service with shared riding, vertiport-airspace capacity, and recharge. Transportation Research Part E, 2025.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tre.2025.104548  
  用途：同时考虑垂直起降场容量、空域容量与充电约束的 UAM 调度，案例侧建模细节参照。

- `Preis L, Hornung M. Vertiport Operations Modeling, Agent-Based Simulation and Parameter Value Specification. Electronics, 2022, 11(7): 1071.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics11071071  
  用途：垂直起降场 Agent 仿真与参数取值，直接对应你 F0–F4 里的细节参数。

- `Nagrare S R, Lieb T J. Throughput and Capacity Analysis of a Vertiport with Taxing and Parking Levels. Aerospace, 2026, 13(1): 109.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/aerospace13010109  
  用途：垂直起降场滑行/停放/充电层级对吞吐能力的影响，适合“细节保留到哪一层”的对照。

- `Doctor F, Budd T, Williams P D, Prescott M, Iqbal R. Modelling the effect of electric aircraft on airport operations and infrastructure. Technological Forecasting and Social Change, 2022.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.techfore.2022.121553  
  用途：电动飞机对机场运营与基础设施影响的建模，交代机场侧研究基础。

- `Energy, power, and infrastructure demands from electrifying airport ground support equipment at United States airports. Nature Communications, 2026, 17: 4612.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-71125-4  
  用途：顶刊机场地面保障设备电动化的充电负荷与基础设施需求，方法上可对比“简化 vs 精细”建模。
  备注：作者名单请按原文补全。

- `Taye S, et al. Energy Demand Analysis for eVTOL Charging Stations in Urban Air Mobility. AIAA 2024-4627, 2024.`  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.2514/6.2024-4627  
  用途：eVTOL 充电站能量需求分析的早期会议论文，用于展示该问题正在形成研究线。

## 6. 优先精读顺序

1. `Priesmann et al., 2019`：学“复杂度—精度”怎么量化、怎么下结论。
2. `Wirtz et al., 2021`：学“最低够用复杂度”判定标准怎么写。
3. `Peinturier & Wallom, 2025`：学用敏感度分析回答“哪些简化可接受”。
4. `Wu et al., 2025 (TRC)`：学 TRC 对 UAM 充电负荷的写作格式与定位。
5. `Fan et al., 2026 (eTransportation)`：对照低空经济能源侧的最新进展。
6. `Preis & Hornung, 2022`：理解垂直起降场内部细节参数如何影响充电与吞吐。
7. `Weng et al., 2025 (TRC)`：补低空交通管理的写作框架。
